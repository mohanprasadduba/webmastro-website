Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will an agent get back to me?

Within one business day for any inquiry submitted through the site, and usually the same day for scheduling a viewing.

Do I need to be pre-approved before viewing a property?

Not to view, but we'll ask for a pre-approval letter before submitting an offer on your behalf — it strengthens the offer and speeds up the process.

What does it cost to list a property with Meridian?

Commission is discussed and agreed before your property goes live — there's no fee for the initial consultation or valuation walkthrough.

Can I schedule a viewing outside business hours?

Most of our agents can accommodate early morning or evening viewings with a little notice — just mention it when you reach out.

Do you handle rentals as well as sales?

Yes — the same listing and viewing process applies whether a property is for sale or for rent.

What happens after I submit a property to list?

An agent visits in person to photograph and walk the property before it goes live — we don't publish a listing from photos alone.
