Our story

Built one small batch at a time.

Hearth started as a single roasting drum in the back of a bakery and grew into a room people now treat like a second living room. What began as a way to keep the bakery's own coffee interesting turned into a full coffeehouse, one regular at a time.

We roast in small batches, pull every shot to order, and keep the pastry case short so everything on it is worth finishing. Nothing about the room is meant to rush you — the chairs are the kind you sink into, and the playlist stays quiet enough to hear the person across the table.
