Custom Tank Fabrication

A processing plant needed a tank geometry that didn't match anything in a standard catalog — an irregular footprint dictated by an existing structural column two other shops weren't willing to design around. We took the site measurements ourselves rather than working from someone else's drawing.

“Delivered on schedule, no change orders.”

The tank was built in three sections to clear a low doorway on site, then welded and pressure-tested as a single unit before installation. Every seam was inspected against ASME code before it left the shop, not after a complaint came back.

Total turnaround from signed drawing to installed tank was six weeks, including a two-week lead time on the stainless stock. The plant has since brought us two more projects on the strength of this one.
