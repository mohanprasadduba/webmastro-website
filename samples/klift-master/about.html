The studio

Who we are

An interior & architecture consultancy based in Italy.

We're a small studio that takes on a deliberately small number of projects at a time. Every plan starts with how the space will actually be lived in — the rest, materials, lighting, furniture, follows from that.

We work closely with each client from first sketch to final walkthrough, rather than handing a finished plan across a table. That's slower, and it's why the studio stays small.
